DishAI Clean Privacy Policy

Effective October 23, 2026

On-device analysis

DishAI Clean analyzes dishwasher and item photos on your device. The developer does not receive your photos or analysis results. The app has no advertising, analytics, tracking, or developer-hosted accounts.

Camera and selected photos

Camera permission lets you photograph a rack or item. When you select an existing photo, the operating system provides the selected image for local analysis. Photo analysis does not upload images to an AI service.

Local history and settings

Scan history, saved thumbnails, dishwasher preferences, progress, trial state, and access status are stored locally. You can delete individual scans from History. Removing the app removes its local app files; operating-system backups may retain earlier copies according to your device settings. Android app backup is disabled. Trial or purchase entitlement records may persist separately through platform storage or the app store.

Sharing is your choice

If you choose Share, DishAI Clean passes a generated result card to the operating system share sheet. The destination app or person you choose receives that shared content under their own privacy practices. The developer does not receive it.

Purchases

Apple App Store or Google Play handles the optional one-time purchase and restoration. The app checks purchase status using the platform billing service. The developer does not receive payment card details. The optional three-day trial is managed locally and does not automatically charge you or create a subscription.

Optional reminders

If you enable reminders and allow notifications, the app schedules local notifications on your device. Reminders do not use a developer server or analytics. You can turn notifications off in device settings.

Support and external links

If you email support, we receive the message and any information you choose to include so we can respond. Do not include sensitive photos or payment details. Opening a policy or support link uses your browser; the hosting provider may process standard web request information.

Your choices and contact

You can revoke camera or notification permission in device settings, delete saved scans, decline sharing, or uninstall the app. For privacy questions, contact rizkcorsight@rizkcorsight.com.
