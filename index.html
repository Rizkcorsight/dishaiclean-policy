Get help with rack guidance, item checks, purchases, or privacy.

Contact us

Email rizkcorsight@rizkcorsight.com. Include your device model, operating-system version, app version, and the steps that led to the issue. Please do not send payment details or sensitive photos.

Getting a useful scan

Use a clear, well-lit photo of the rack or item. Retake blurry or angled images. Guidance is an estimate: follow the item manufacturer's care instructions and your dishwasher manual. An uncertain result does not establish that an item is dishwasher-safe.

Purchases and trial

The three-day trial is managed in the app and does not automatically charge you. Full access is an optional one-time purchase. Use Restore purchases on the paywall with the same store account used for the purchase. Apple and Google handle payments and refund requests.

History and reminders

Delete saved scans from History. Manage camera and notification permission in device settings. Analysis happens on your device; you choose whether to export a result using Share.
